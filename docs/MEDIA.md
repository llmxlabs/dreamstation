## Generation prompts

All images used built-in ImageGen; no API/CLI fallback was used.

- **Concept:** A premium widescreen editorial product image for DreamStation. Minimal dark living room at twilight, one large slim wall-mounted display showing an original cyan/violet nebula over a tranquil alien ocean; concrete and walnut, photographic materials, quiet cinematic lighting. No people, text, logos, fictitious UI or extra screens. Landscape 16:9.
- **Nebula sea:** A cinematic alien seascape at blue hour; immense translucent cyan nebula above a mirror-still ocean, violet celestial clouds and small moons, dark island spires and luminous particles. Calm fine-art composition in midnight blue, teal and purple. Edge-to-edge 16:9; no people, lettering, watermarks, frame or room.
- **Desert portal:** A monumental circular ivory-travertine portal in apricot desert dunes, a still reflecting pool, distant rose mountains and a warm setting sun. Tactile surreal fine art with a minimalist photographic composition, amber/peach palette. Edge-to-edge 16:9; no people, text, logos or frame.
- **Moonlit forest:** Bioluminescent woodland with a narrow glassy stream, ancient trees, emerald/turquoise light, suspended fireflies, misty moonbeams, ferns and moss. Cinematic fine-art environment, deep greens and gold accents. Edge-to-edge 16:9; no people, text, watermark, border or room.
